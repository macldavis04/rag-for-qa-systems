Tesla, Inc. was founded in July 2003 by Martin Eberhard and Marc Tarpenning.
Elon Musk joined Tesla in 2004 as chairman and later became CEO in 2008.
Tesla's mission is to accelerate the world's transition to sustainable energy.
The Tesla Roadster, released in 2008, was the company's first production vehicle.
The Tesla Model S is a full-size luxury sedan with a top speed exceeding 200 mph and one of the longest ranges of any electric car.
The Tesla Model 3 was designed to be a more affordable, mass-market electric vehicle with a starting price under $40,000.
The Tesla Model X features distinctive falcon-wing doors and seating for up to seven passengers.
The Tesla Model Y is a compact SUV sharing many parts with the smaller sedan, built for buyers who need more cargo space.
Tesla operates a network of Superchargers for fast charging on long trips.
Tesla's Gigafactories manufacture batteries and vehicles at large scale.
The Gigafactory Nevada primarily produces battery cells and packs.
Tesla Energy sells solar panels and the Powerwall home battery system.
Autopilot is Tesla's driver assistance system included in most vehicles.
Full Self-Driving, or FSD, is an additional package Tesla offers for advanced automation features.
Tesla is headquartered in Austin, Texas, after relocating from Palo Alto, California in 2021.
Tesla went public on the NASDAQ stock exchange in June 2010.
The Cybertruck is Tesla's all-electric pickup truck with an angular stainless steel design.
Tesla vehicles use large touchscreen displays as the primary interface for most controls.
